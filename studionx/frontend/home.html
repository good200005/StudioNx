What Studio Nx is, and who it's for

What is Studio Nx?

Studio Nx is a DAW sequencer that runs entirely in your browser. Drop in your samples, write melodies on the piano roll or with a MIDI keyboard, shape the sound with web-native synths and effects, and export a finished WAV or MP3 — nothing to install.

Stuck on the mix? Hand it a track you love. The AI listens to that reference and suggests synth presets, mixing moves and a mastering chain you can apply — and undo — in one click. Invite friends to the same project and build it together in real time, while Nyxi keeps the beat.

Who it's for

  • First-time producers

    Never opened a DAW? Start making music in a browser tab. When the mix sounds off, the AI tells you what to change and why.

  • Beat makers

    Build from samples. Your library lives in your account, so the same project opens on whichever computer you sit down at.

  • Bands & study groups

    Stop emailing project files. Invite your crew, edit the same session at once, and talk it through in the project chat.
