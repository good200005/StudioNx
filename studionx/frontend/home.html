Service

What Studio Nx is, and who it's for

What is Studio Nx?

Studio Nx is a DAW sequencer that runs entirely in your browser. Drop in your samples, write melodies on the piano roll or with a MIDI keyboard, shape the sound with web-native synths and effects, and export a finished WAV or MP3, with nothing to install.

Stuck on the mix? Hand it a track you love. The AI listens to that reference and suggests synth presets, mixing moves and a mastering chain you can apply, and undo, in one click. Invite friends to the same project and build it together in real time, while Nyxi keeps the beat.

Who it's for

  • First-time producers

    Never opened a DAW? Start making music in a browser tab. When the mix sounds off, the AI tells you what to change and why.

  • Beat makers

    Build from samples. Your library lives in your account, so the same project opens on whichever computer you sit down at.

  • Bands & study groups

    Stop emailing project files. Invite your crew, edit the same session at once, and talk it through in the project chat.

Character

Meet Nyxi

Nyxi — a child of the night, and of unfinished songs

Nyxi was born from Nyx, the goddess of night, out of the sounds that never sleep: melodies hummed at 3 a.m. and songs that never got an ending. Abandoned songs fade into the night sky. Nyxi came down to Studio Nx to make sure yours doesn't.

The crescent pin in her hair is a sliver of the moon. It grows a little with every song you finish. It hasn't been full yet. She acts like she couldn't care less, but she never gives up on a song she's heard, and she won't give up on you either.

“A silent night is too quiet. …So go make some noise.”

Feature

What you can make with it

  • A full sequencer in a browser tab

    Drop samples from your desktop into your own library, draw notes on the piano roll or play them in on a MIDI keyboard, and shape them with web-native synths and effects. Export to WAV or MP3 when it's done.

  • Start from a track you love

    Paste a YouTube link or upload a file, pick a genre, and the AI reads its tempo, key, tone and loudness. It suggests synth presets, a mix and a mastering chain you can apply, and undo, in one click.

  • Make it together, live

    Invite your crew to the same project and edit at once, like a shared doc. See who is working on which track, leave comments on the timeline, and talk it through in the project chat.
