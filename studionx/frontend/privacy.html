1. Who we are

Studio Nx is run by an independent developer (“we”). We decide how your personal information is used and are responsible for it — the controller under the GDPR and the personal information handler under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account — until you delete it.
  • After you delete your account — you can't sign in from that moment and your profile photo is deleted. We keep your email and nickname for 3 days so they can't be taken over straight away, then remove them automatically.
  • Projects you created — stay available to the people you worked with and show you as “Deleted user”.
  • Messages you send us — 1 year after we reply. If we never reply, 1 year after you send them.
  • Error reports — deleted automatically by Sentry when its retention period ends.

When we delete data, we delete it in a way that can't be recovered.

6. We don't sell your data

We don't sell your personal information and don't share it for targeted advertising — including as those terms are defined under California law (CCPA/CPRA).

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See the information we hold about you, and get a copy in a common format you can take elsewhere
  • Correct it — your nickname and photo can be changed any time on your Profile page
  • Delete it — use Delete account at the bottom of your Profile page
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Withdraw consent you've given, for example for a message you sent us

Email us (section 12) and we'll reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. You can also complain to a data protection authority — in Korea the Personal Information Protection Commission, in the EU or UK your local authority.

8. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Short-lived sign-in tokens stay in page memory; the long-lived one is in a cookie scripts can't read.
  • The live site uses HTTPS only.
  • Only administrator accounts can open the admin area.
  • Passwords, tokens and cookies are removed before error reports leave our server.

9. Children

You must be 16 or older to create an account. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we'll delete it.

10. If something goes wrong

If your personal information is leaked or accessed without permission, we'll tell you and the relevant authority within 72 hours of finding out, together with what happened and what we're doing about it.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post any change on the Development notes on our home page at least 7 days before it takes effect. If a change affects what you agreed to, we'll ask you again.
